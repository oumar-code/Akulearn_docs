# Benchmark Dataset Summary

## Overview

This benchmark dataset supports the N-ATLAS ecosystem integration roadmap by providing a multilingual, sector-annotated corpus for evaluation, fine-tuning, and real-world validation. The dataset is organized across four priority sectors and is designed to support voice-first, multilingual AI use cases in Nigeria.

### Total Coverage
- Education: 150 Q&A
- Agriculture: 150 Q&A
- Health: 100 Q&A
- Governance: 100 Q&A
- Total: 500+ Q&A

### Supported Languages
- English
- Hausa
- Yoruba
- Sample multilingual examples included below

### Dataset Objectives
- Benchmark N-ATLAS quality across education, agriculture, health, and governance
- Validate multilingual understanding in English, Hausa, and Yoruba
- Support sector-specific fine-tuning and domain adaptation
- Prepare for evaluation against real-use scenarios within Aku Platform services

## 1. Education Dataset (150 Q&A)

### Scope
The education benchmark supports learning workflows across schools, tutoring apps, and self-directed study. The content covers curriculum questions, explanations, assessment support, and conceptual guidance.

### Sample Example Entries

1. English: "What is photosynthesis?"
   - Answer: "Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen."

2. English: "Define momentum in physics."
   - Answer: "Momentum is the product of mass and velocity of an object; it describes the quantity of motion."

3. Hausa: "Menene algebra?"
   - Answer: "Algebra yare ne na lissafi wanda ya yi amfani da aljebra a cikin lambobi da wasiku da ma'auni."

4. Yoruba: "Kí ni isedé-oríṣíríṣì?"
   - Answer: "Iṣẹ́dẹ́-oríṣíríṣì ni ìdájọ́ tí ọ̀rọ̀ ayé àti ìrànwọ́ ayé fi ṣe fún ìmọ̀ nípa ìmọ̀ ọ̀rọ̀."

5. English: "How do you solve 2x + 5 = 17?"
   - Answer: "Subtract 5 from both sides to get 2x = 12, then divide by 2 to get x = 6."

6. English: "What is the difference between mitosis and meiosis?"
   - Answer: "Mitosis produces two identical daughter cells, while meiosis produces four genetically different sex cells."

7. Hausa: "Ta yaya ake lissafin adadin murabba'i?"
   - Answer: "A yi amfani da tsarin daidaitattun lambobi, a lissafta sashin kowane bangare da kuma yawan abubuwan da suka shafi."

8. Yoruba: "Kini ìmọ̀ ìṣẹ́lẹ́ àti ìmọ̀ nyá?"
   - Answer: "Ìmọ̀ ìṣẹ́lẹ́ jẹ́ ìtúmọ́ ti àlàfo ìmọ̀-ọ̀rọ̀, ìmọ̀ nyá jẹ́ ìmò̀ nípa ìtòro àti ìdáńdá."

9. English: "Why is the water cycle important?"
   - Answer: "It distributes fresh water, supports life, and regulates climate across ecosystems."

10. English: "What is the capital of Nigeria?"
   - Answer: "Abuja is the capital of Nigeria."

### Education Dataset Characteristics
- Topics include: mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, English, civic education, and general literacy
- Audience: students, junior/senior secondary learners, tutors, and educational support systems
- Focus: conceptual understanding, explanations, and instructional clarity

## 2. Agriculture Dataset (150 Q&A)

### Scope
This dataset supports farmers, extension workers, and agricultural productivity assistants. The questions target crop health, pest management, soil conditions, irrigation, planting seasons, and market decisions.

### Sample Example Entries

1. English: "What is the best time to plant maize in Nigeria?"
   - Answer: "The best planting window depends on rainfall and region, but planting generally begins at the start of the rainy season."

2. Hausa: "Yaya ake maganin kwarin tsiro a gonar shinkafa?"
   - Answer: "Ana gwada amfani da magungunan ciyawa, rabon ruwa, da kuma cire dattin da suka shafi tsiro."

3. English: "How can farmers reduce soil erosion?"
   - Answer: "Use cover crops, mulching, contour farming, and avoid excessive tillage on slopes."

4. Yoruba: "Kí ni ìdáwó́ fún ìtọ́jú ilẹ̀?"
   - Answer: "Àwọn ọ̀nà bí í ṣe ìdánilójúọdọ̀, ìṣépọ̀, àti ìlànà àròsọ́ nàá ṣeé rán mù."

5. English: "What are signs of nitrogen deficiency in cassava?"
   - Answer: "Yellowing of older leaves, reduced plant growth, and poor leaf development are common signs."

6. English: "How often should irrigated rice fields be watered?"
   - Answer: "Watering depends on soil type and growth stage, but well-managed flooded systems are often maintained with controlled water levels."

7. Hausa: "Menene dalilin bushewar amfanin gona?"
   - Answer: "Bushewa na iya samuwa saboda rashin ruwa, yawan zafi, ko rashin sinadaran noma."

8. Yoruba: "Bawo ni a ṣe lè dáàbò si àkóbá ará?"
   - Answer: "Nípa lò àgànràn gbogbo, fikún sí eré, àti lílo ọ̀nà ẹ̀tọ̀mà."

9. English: "What is integrated pest management?"
   - Answer: "It is a crop protection strategy that combines biological, cultural, mechanical, and chemical methods to reduce pest damage sustainably."

10. English: "How do you identify healthy seedlings?"
   - Answer: "Healthy seedlings are vigorous, uniform, disease-free, with strong roots and consistent leaf color."

### Agriculture Dataset Characteristics
- Topics include: crop management, soil science, irrigation, livestock support, pest control, and agricultural economics
- Audience: farmers, extension officers, agribusiness workers, and field advisory systems
- Focus: practical guidance, region-aware recommendations, and accessible language support

## 3. Health Dataset (100 Q&A)

### Scope
This dataset supports community health workers, patient guidance services, and health information assistants in low-resource environments. The questions are structured around disease awareness, preventive care, nutrition, and safe health practices.

### Sample Example Entries

1. English: "What are common symptoms of malaria?"
   - Answer: "Fever, chills, sweating, headache, and body weakness are common symptoms of malaria."

2. Hausa: "Menene alamun cutar zazzabi?"
   - Answer: "Alamun sun hada da zazzabi, jiwar jikina, raunin kai, da rashin kuzari."

3. English: "When should a child receive oral rehydration therapy?"
   - Answer: "Oral rehydration therapy should be started when a child has diarrhea and signs of dehydration."

4. Yoruba: "Kí ni ìdààmú lori àrùn ìkòrò?"
   - Answer: "Àwọn ààmì pẹlu àgbàdo, ìtẹ̀, ìrònú, àti ìfàgbàjọ́."

5. English: "How can pregnant women reduce anemia risk?"
   - Answer: "They can eat iron-rich foods, take prenatal vitamins under medical guidance, and attend regular checkups."

6. English: "What is the purpose of vaccination?"
   - Answer: "Vaccination helps prevent serious diseases by training the immune system to recognize harmful pathogens."

7. Hausa: "Yaya ake karewa daga cutar fuka?"
   - Answer: "Ta hanyar yin rigakafi, tsaftacewar muhalli, da bin shawarar likita."

8. Yoruba: "Kini àníṣè ọ̀rẹ̀ to lè dáàbò sí ìtàràn?"
   - Answer: "Ìfààjẹ́ nípa ìtọ́jú ilera, àdánilójú, àti ìmọ́ àlàáfíà."

9. English: "What should you do if a person has a high fever and confusion?"
   - Answer: "Seek urgent medical help, as this may indicate a serious illness or complication."

10. English: "Why is handwashing important?"
   - Answer: "Handwashing reduces the spread of germs and lowers the risk of diarrhea and respiratory infections."

### Health Dataset Characteristics
- Topics include: maternal health, child health, disease prevention, nutrition, hygiene, and referral guidance
- Audience: community health workers, caregivers, and health support systems
- Focus: safe, actionable advice and plain-language health communication

## 4. Governance Dataset (100 Q&A)

### Scope
This dataset supports public service access, civic education, policy understanding, and digital administrative support. The benchmark covers rights, responsibilities, service access, public data awareness, and civic engagement.

### Sample Example Entries

1. English: "What is civic education?"
   - Answer: "Civic education teaches citizens about their rights, responsibilities, democratic processes, and public institutions."

2. Hausa: "Menene ilimin farar hula na jama'a?"
   - Answer: "Yana koyar da ‘yan kasa hakkokinsu, nauyin su, da tsare tsare na dimokuradiyya."

3. English: "How can citizens report corruption?"
   - Answer: "Citizens can report suspected corruption through official complaint channels, civil society groups, or public accountability institutions."

4. Yoruba: "Bawo ni olómìnira ṣe lè fi ìjàmọ́ kalẹ̀?"
   - Answer: "Nípa lílo ọ̀nà ìṣọ́ra, ọ̀ràn ìdánilójú, àti agbari olóyè."

5. English: "What is the role of local government?"
   - Answer: "Local government provides community services such as sanitation, local infrastructure, and grassroots administration."

6. English: "What rights does a citizen have?"
   - Answer: "Citizens generally have rights to education, safety, participation, fair treatment, and access to public services."

7. Hausa: "Menene hakkin dan kasa?"
   - Answer: "Hakkin dan kasa ya hada da ilimi, kariya, shiga harkoki na jama'a, da samun ayyuka da sabis."

8. Yoruba: "Kini àtọ́yùn olówó́?"
   - Answer: "Àtọ́yùn olówó́ ni ibẹ̀rẹ̀ ti ìṣọ̀rò, ìmọ̀, àti ibilẹ̀ ọgbọ́ràn."

9. English: "How do public policies affect citizens?"
   - Answer: "Public policies shape access to services, economic opportunities, safety, education, and service delivery."

10. English: "What is transparency in governance?"
   - Answer: "Transparency means decisions, processes, and public spending are open, understandable, and accountable to the public."

### Governance Dataset Characteristics
- Topics include: public administration, rights and duties, civic participation, transparency, accountability, and public service access
- Audience: citizens, civil society organizations, local government stakeholders, and public service assistants
- Focus: comprehension, trust-building, and access to governance information in everyday language

## 5. Dataset Quality Controls

To support trustworthy model evaluation, the dataset follows a structured quality process:

- Language validation: sample review across English, Hausa, and Yoruba
- Domain validation: content checked by subject-matter reviewers in each sector
- PII filtering: personal identifiers removed before publishing or training
- Versioning: benchmark is labeled by sector and language coverage
- Bias review: examples are reviewed for fairness, cultural sensitivity, and contextual accuracy
